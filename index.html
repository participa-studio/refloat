ReFloat

What boats leave behind.

ReFloat turns end-of-life boats into a school, a workshop and a shop, in that order.

Mission

We care for our coast locally, we teach what the ocean receives, we take direct action on what boats leave behind, and we count the impact.

What it is

ReFloat is an Open Coastal Laboratory.

It is a continuous social design project: a place for raising awareness of ocean impact, open to collect, co-repair, co-transform and recycle boats and their gear, on and off the water, with the people who live alongside them.

It holds three priorities equally: financially self-sustaining, socially positive and environmentally sustainable.

How an object moves through ReFloat

  • Raise awareness
  • Collect
  • Repair
  • Transform
  • Recycle

A boat that can still sail is kept sailing first.

Four pillars

  1. Ocean education (lead)Schools visit the workshop to see what a boat leaves behind. Each dismantled boat becomes a case: its story, its materials, and where its waste would have gone.
  2. Reuse and repurposeTested equipment resold as it is; sails, canvas and expired safety gear made into new products.
  3. Learning through workSupervised trainees, at a distance from the labour market, learning repair and repurposing.
  4. Responsible disposalOne door for an end-of-life boat: equipment harvested, hull sent to a certified partner, certificate issued.

Where it starts

The Netherlands first, beginning in a neighbourhood maker space in Amsterdam. Portugal and Brazil are being explored.

Status

Early stage, built in the open. This page records the concept as it stood on 2 November 2026.
