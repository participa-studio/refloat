# ReFloat — brand decisions

Recorded: 3 November 2026
Decided by: Bernardo van de Schepop, Participa Studio (Haarlem, Netherlands)

These decisions were made before, and independently of, any course or institutional work. They are recorded here so that their date is public.

## Name

ReFloat

## Tagline

What boats leave behind.

The tagline explains why the work matters.

## Descriptor

Open Coastal Laboratory

The descriptor explains what the space is. It is used in body copy, website bios, grant proposals and field-guide headlines. It is not part of the logo or the medallion.

## Lockup

- Heavy-duty, unembellished "gear" aesthetic.
- Split weight: either "RE" bold and "float" light, or the reverse. The split expresses industrial transformation and structural salvage.
- Which of the two weight orders is used is still open.

## Medallion (the seal)

A flat, two-dimensional geometric stamp. Used on intake plates, certificates and workshop signage.

- Outer ring: "REFLOAT" arched across the top and "WHAT BOATS LEAVE BEHIND" around the bottom, separated by simple mechanical rivets.
- No place names. The brand stays borderless, like a vessel.
- Centre: a simplified, bold, deconstructed boat hull or sail breaking apart, standing for dissection, recycling and teaching. No fine detail.

## Status

The lockup and the medallion are described here as decisions of direction. Final artwork does not yet exist.
